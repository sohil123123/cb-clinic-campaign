AI PRECISION RECOVERY

What’s driving your pain?
Is treatment helping?

Your physiotherapist uses AI to bring your symptoms and test results together, then tracks how you respond to treatment.

01

Pinpoint what limits your movement

AI helps organise your history, strength readings and movement tests. Your physiotherapist checks which findings explain your symptoms and records the likely cause in your report.

02

Turn findings into a personal milestone

If stairs hurt, your physio checks what limits you today and agrees a goal with you, such as climbing one flight comfortably. That goal guides your exercises and treatment.

03

See what changes between visits

Your care team compares repeat tests with your first visit and asks how stairs feel now. If progress stalls, they reassess the cause and adjust your treatment.

Ask our team on WhatsApp

Will I need every machine or therapy?

No. Your physiotherapist recommends treatments after assessment. A modality may be included when appropriate; your plan can also include guided exercise and hands-on care. Confirm equipment availability at your preferred centre.

How is AI used in my care?

AI assists with structured assessment and treatment planning. Your physiotherapist reviews the information, decides what is appropriate and remains responsible for your clinical care.

What will my first visit involve?

Your ₹499 clinic assessment (usually ₹999) includes a discussion of your symptoms and history, relevant movement and strength checks, and an AI-assisted assessment report reviewed by your physiotherapist. You will discuss goals and recommended next steps. Bring existing reports if you have them.

How much does a session cost?

Assessment & consultation is ₹499 on the current offer, usually ₹999, including an AI-assisted assessment report. Standard physiotherapy is ₹999 per session; advanced physiotherapy is ₹1,499 per session. Your physiotherapist recommends suitable care after assessment. These are clinic fees; home-care fees are confirmed separately.

How many sessions will I need?

This varies with your condition, goals and response to treatment. Your physiotherapist will discuss a plan after assessment and review it with your progress.

Can I book for an injury or after surgery?

Yes. Tell the team what you need when booking, and bring relevant medical or surgery notes to help plan your rehabilitation.

Is my appointment confirmed when I send a request?

An appointment request is not a confirmed slot. The team confirms your centre, date and time. For an immediate conversation, call or continue on WhatsApp.
