Before you come

Do I need a doctor's referral?

No. You can book directly. If you already have a prescription, scans or surgery notes, bring them and your physiotherapist will work from them.

Can I come today?

Usually yes. Call or request a call back and we will give you the next free slot. Evenings fill first, so for a same-day visit a morning or early afternoon slot is more likely.

How many sessions will I need?

You will get an estimate at the end of the first visit. Most problems settle in six to ten sessions; post-surgery rehab follows the surgeon's timeline.

Will insurance cover it?

Clinic physiotherapy is usually paid directly and claimed later where your policy allows outpatient care. You get a receipt and a report after every visit for that.

Can the physiotherapist visit me at home?

Yes, for people who cannot travel after surgery or with severe pain. Home visits cost more and have fewer time slots, so mention it when you call.
